Commissioned to test site feasibility for Complying Development (CDC) on a steep, challenging parcel in The Hill, Newcastle. 

The design demonstrates that dual occupancies can achieve supreme architectural individuality, generous private open space, and abundant natural light through thoughtful topographical stepping.

### Design Highlights
- **Solar Optimization:** Winter solstice shadow analysis proved comprehensive 3-hour minimum solar access across all main living zones and private courtyards.
- **Topographical Stepping:** Split-level layout minimizes cut-and-fill, creating soaring ceiling heights in primary living areas while providing basement garaging.
