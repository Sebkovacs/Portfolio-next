### The Problem
Achieving high-density sustainable living on a tight, expensive coastal suburban block without sacrificing the relaxed, outdoor-focused coastal vibe. The project needed to hit stringent Passive House (Passivhaus) standards to minimize energy use, while still accommodating the messy, sandy realities of a surf lifestyle.

### Client Profile
Two traveling families, close friends who decided to pool their resources to purchase a single suburban block near Bar Beach. They needed two separate, private dwellings that could share a communal outdoor space, prioritizing environmental sustainability and community over sprawling private square meterage.

### Sustainable Coastal Density
The Boardrider’s Duplex proves that medium-density coastal living does not need to rely on generic, high-carbon construction methods.

- **High-Performance Shell:** The primary wall structure is constructed from Hempcrete—a highly insulating, carbon-negative bio-composite that naturally regulates humidity and temperature.
- **Passive House Standards:** A continuous airtight envelope and mechanical Heat Recovery Ventilation (HRV) ensure perfect indoor air quality and constant thermal comfort with virtually zero heating or cooling bills.
- **Communal Flow:** The traditional suburban backyard fence is removed. Instead, the dwellings share a central landscaped courtyard featuring a communal outdoor shower, expansive surfboard storage, and a productive kitchen garden.
