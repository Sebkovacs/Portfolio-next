An off-grid rural and coastal retreat in the Seal Rocks hinterland, designed as a secondary eco-dwelling celebrating sustainable simplicity, natural timber finishes, and complete water and energy autonomy.

### Key Architectural Strategies
- **Passive Thermal Zoning:** Living areas orientate North-East to capture winter sun and offshore breezes while protected from harsh westerly heat.
- **Owner-Builder Feasibility:** Standardized structural grid and local Australian timber framing engineered for cost-effective construction and minimal site disturbance.
- **Closed-Loop Systems:** Rainwater harvesting, dry composting, and standalone solar power.
