An architectural renovation study testing two distinct spatial concepts for a compact coastal townhouse in Cooks Hill, Newcastle.

### Concept Innovations
- **Light Well & Stack Cooling:** Concept 1 reconfigured the central stair core beneath an expansive double skylight, creating a thermal chimney that draws cool ground-level air up and out during summer.
- **Raked Volume & Exposed Timber:** Concept 2 opened the upper ceiling to the structural rafters, doubling perceived volume and providing high-level louvre ventilation for maximum sea-breeze airflow.
