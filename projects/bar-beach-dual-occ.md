Situated in Newcastle’s prestigious Bar Beach precinct, this project explores high-density coastal living without sacrificing architectural individuality, privacy, or generous garden connections. 

The design accommodates two independent family homes under one unified architectural expression. Aimed at multi-generational living and shared coastal property investment, the project demonstrates how density can be achieved with sensitivity and high-end detailing.

### Dual Identity & Shared Vernacular
The architectural language is rooted in a heavy, natural Sydney sandstone base, establishing a permanent, grounded relationship with the site. Above this masonry podium, the upper levels are articulated with lightweight, warm vertical timber batten screening. This operable skin allows residents to continually negotiate their relationship with the coastal sun, prevailing breezes, and neighborhood sightlines.

### Architectural Innovations & Planning
Designing two high-performance homes on a single suburban lot required meticulous planning and spatial negotiation.
- **Acoustic & Visual Privacy:** Carefully angled view corridors, offset courtyards, and solid masonry party-walls ensure complete acoustic isolation and visual privacy between the two residences.
- **Solar Optimization:** Both dwellings achieve over 4.5 hours of direct winter solar access to primary living zones, a complex achievement on a subdivided site.
- **Durable Coastal Materials:** Hand-pitched sandstone, anodised marine aluminum window suites, and native hardwood decks ensure longevity with minimal maintenance.
- **DA Pathway Mastery:** The project represents a successful navigation of complex local planning controls, achieving full height compliance and zero objection resolution through the City of Newcastle.
