Perched high on the Merewether headland, Salt Point House is an architectural investigation into coastal permanence, passive environmental performance, and the daily rituals of ocean living. 

Designed for a family deeply immersed in surfing and coastal life, the residence abandons traditional lightweight beach house vernacular in favour of monolithic, monumental forms. It is anchored by 400mm stabilised rammed-earth spine walls that shield private courtyard zones from harsh southerly gales while opening completely to the north-eastern sun and offshore morning swells.

### The Site Context & Topography
Navigating a steep, geotechnically challenging coastal hazard zone required precision engineering. The structure is anchored deeply into the escarpment bedrock, allowing the primary living volume to cantilever outwards, hovering above the native coastal scrub. This elevated datum not only secures panoramic sightlines up the coastline but also allows heavy storm run-off to pass uninterrupted beneath the dwelling.

### Material Philosophy: Weathering Gracefully
The material palette was selected specifically for its ability to patina under extreme salt-spray exposure. 
- **Silvertop Ash:** Externally, the timber cladding is left raw to silver off, blending the structure into the coastal heath. 
- **Rammed Earth:** Internally, the earth walls provide a warm, tactile counterpoint to the expansive ocean views, their striated layers reflecting the sedimentary cliffs of the headland itself.
- **Raw Copper:** Copper flashings and custom rain showers will oxidize over time, telling the story of the home's interaction with the coastal atmosphere.

### Passive Solar & High-Performance Engineering
The project operates 100% off-grid, a significant achievement for a luxury headland residence.
- **Thermal Inertia:** The rammed-earth walls absorb winter sun deep into the floor plate during the day, radiating warmth throughout the evening and entirely eliminating the need for artificial heating.
- **Natural Stack Ventilation:** High-level operable clerestory glazing induces natural convection. As cool sea breezes are drawn across the ocean deck, warm internal air is flushed out, naturally cooling the home during intense summer months.
- **Surf Ritual Flow:** A carefully considered entry sequence caters to the family's daily ocean immersion. Direct sandy-feet access from the cliff path leads into an open-air copper rain shower, a timber board drying bay, and seamlessly transitions into the family living wing without tracking sand through the home.
