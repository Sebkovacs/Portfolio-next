---

## Practice Vision
Salt & Stone Architecture is a Newcastle-based architectural practice dedicated to bespoke coastal dwellings, headland pavilions, and off-grid sanctuaries across New South Wales.

Founded by Sebastian Kovacs — a Registered Architect and lifelong surfer with a young family — the studio designs homes deeply connected to ocean swells, passive solar thermal mass, marine-resilient materiality, and the daily rituals of coastal living.

---

## Core Capabilities
- **Coastal Headland & Dune Pavilions:** Sensitive site planning, wind deflection, and passive solar orientation.
- **Ultra-Sustainable & Off-Grid Living:** Stabilised rammed earth, sandstone thermal mass, solar microgrids, and rainwater harvesting.
- **Planning Approvals & Coastal Councils:** DA and CDC pathways, Clause 4.6 variations, and coastal hazard assessments (City of Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Port Stephens, MidCoast, Byron Shire).
- **Precision Technical BIM:** 100% Revit modeling, marine-grade fixture specifications, and tender-ready construction documentation.

## Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack)
- **Language:** TypeScript 5
- **Styling:** Vanilla CSS with custom architectural design tokens (`styles/theme.css`)
- **Content:** Markdown with `gray-matter`, `remark`, and `remark-html`
- **Dynamic Features:**
  - Interactive Architectural Plan & CAD Drawing Viewer with multi-sheet tabs and fullscreen zoom (`components/ArchitecturalPlanViewer.tsx`)
  - Editorial Photography Gallery with fullscreen slideshow carousel (`components/ProjectImageGallery.tsx`)
  - Project Catalogue with dual Visual Grid and Architectural Index Table views (`components/ProjectsCatalogue.tsx`)
  - Dynamic Dark/Light organic coastal theme engine (`components/ThemeProvider.tsx`)
